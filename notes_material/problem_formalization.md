### **Game State**

Most simple setup of Monopoly



##### **Observable**

###### Static

* prices of properties
* prices of houses
* prices for rent
* layout and order of properties
* grouping of properties



###### Dynamic

* which properties are available
* which player owns which properties
* which properties have houses
* how much cash does each player have
* on which fields are each players
* who's turn is it
* what values were rolled
* which players are in the prison and for how many turns already (0-2)
* how often did the current player at turn roll doubles



##### **Not necessarily observable**

* list of event cards
* list of community cards
* all cards and their effects







## **Set of Rules**



Standard Monopoly rules apply, with following specifications:

* no auctions
* no trading and thus bidding
* no mortgage
* loss when not enough cash for rent
